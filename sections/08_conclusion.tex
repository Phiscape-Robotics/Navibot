This draft follows the HLDD structure and records the architecture and software artifacts currently present in the repository. The diagram is preliminary, and the project task, detailed requirements, component-level electrical design, mechanical design, and validation results remain open.

\subsection{Risks and Limitations}
The available artifacts do not establish that the proposed system has been assembled, tested, or validated. Component suitability, performance, safety provisions, and the intended role of the network-discovery script must be confirmed before making operational claims.
