PROJECT BRIEF — NAVIBOT (INITIAL REPOSITORY NOTES)

Confirmed project inputs and repository artifacts
- A Draw.io electronics block diagram and a PNG export are present.
- The diagram depicts a Raspberry Pi 4B, LiDAR, camera, Arduino, L298N dual H-bridge, four DC motors with encoders, an 11.1 V nominal Li-ion battery, and a voltage-regulator/power module.
- The diagram labels UART command/status between the Pi and Arduino, GPIO direction/PWM between the Arduino and motor driver, encoder feedback, and power connections.
- The project uses a Raspberry Pi Camera V2 (IMX219). Its ROS 2 camera_ros setup has been verified on the Pi at 640x480 bgr8 and approximately 15 FPS; calibration remains pending.
- The serial YDLIDAR X2 uses the manufacturer's ROS 2 driver and SDK. Both built on the Pi under ROS 2 Lyrical; a bounded capture produced 30 scans at approximately 11.58 Hz with 260 samples per scan. Physical mounting transform, forward-angle alignment, and ranging accuracy remain unverified.
- The Python script tools/scan_wifi_devices.py performs best-effort IPv4 device discovery on the connected local Wi-Fi/LAN using host network information, ping, and ARP/neighbour data.
- The project owner confirms a front and rear classic Arduino Nano, each controlling two motors, on a shared USB-RS-485 bus connected to the Pi.

Current Raspberry Pi design status
- ROS 2 Lyrical Luth on 64-bit Ubuntu 26.04 LTS arm64 is the proposed Pi runtime; verify the actual Pi board and operating system.
- The main design document, Section 6, records the deployed ROS 2 motor-bridge package, wheel-level ROS topics, Pi Camera V2 bring-up, YDLIDAR X2 bring-up, and the external Nano firmware contract at PROTOCOL.md.
- ROS bridge software and pseudo-terminal integration tests passed, but physical USB-RS-485 communication, Nano firmware, encoder calibration, and motor response remain unverified. Front/rear addresses 1 and 2 and the serial defaults are configured values; adapter turnaround and physical bus behavior still require confirmation.

Treat the diagram as a supplied preliminary architecture artifact, not proof of implementation or compatibility. Record design decisions and assumptions explicitly as they are confirmed. Do not infer a clinical, commercial, or other application from the project name alone.
